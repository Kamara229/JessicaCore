After real physically-paid S7, **three structurally distinct causal relation hypotheses** exist, but their mean gate weights remained approximately 33.33% / 33.33% / 33.33%. Thus S7 did **not** demonstrate that the model knew how to choose the right relation hypothesis.

S8 introduces a separate **opt-in `parallel_selector_version=1`** in the same NRA3 6,106,154-parameter model. The original S7 `parallel_selector_version=0` remains exactly backward-compatible. The new gate combines four differentiable signals, with NO new learned parameters: cosine consistency between the existing paid `rel_q` vector and each hypothesis message; amount of real long-range attention outside the 4-token window; confidence/concentration within the local attention distribution; and role complementarity calculated from NRA3's **own predicted semantic role logits**, not external gold labels.

S8 includes a separate training-only **weak selector teacher** on the registered 12 Russian UD TRAIN sentences. It distinguishes prior complementary semantic roles, currently observable local UD head links (up to 16 native subtokens in S8 to cover Russian wordpieces, while S7's original 4-token window is unchanged), and unresolved/global causal context. These rules are **heuristic training supervision, not truth about the optimal branch**; the model cannot use gold tags or UD arcs at inference and cannot see future-generation tokens. A dependency whose head is to the RIGHT of the current word is never labeled as an already-visible local head. A modest differentiable branch CE term is added to existing grammar, semantics and relational UD-head losses, and gradient flows only into **existing CB59 FP16 K32V8 centroid books and row scales** (unchanged 43 packed 5-bit streams, 1D source intact).
