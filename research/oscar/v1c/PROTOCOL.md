# Oscar v1C — true causal multi-token FP32 teacher distillation in an isolated 12-layer CALIBRI route

## Why
Oscar v1 (#766) attempted shared S142/GT12 gradient training to match independently pretrained 135M FP32; its teacher KL declined but **all 6/8 previously correct original S142 TRAIN answers were destroyed**. Oscar v1B (#767), using a new separate and OFF-by-default rank8 12-layer route, preserved the complete old 8/8 emitted token sequences and 6/8 correct S142 TRAIN answers while teacher-first-token agreement improved **0/8 → 6/8**. Yet teacher-route responses collapsed to single token `F` + EOS, 0/8 complete FP32 responses; v1B optimised FIRST token only. Oscar v1C must train the **whole auto-regressive response**, not rely on first-token metrics.
