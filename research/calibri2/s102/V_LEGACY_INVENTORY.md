## Scope limitations

Directory names and experiment titles are NOT proof of scientific success.
Do not treat all 144 source folders as independent quality demonstrations.
Runs that only pass physical packing, teacher fit, synthetic unit tests,
numeric self-consistency, or QA post-hoc inspection cannot become model
acceptance. The independent V231/V232 questions and S100/S96 questions
are embargoed for all future training and checkpoint selection.

The detailed evidence-to-action findings and selected next hypothesis live
in `V_LEGACY_TRANSFER_AUDIT.md`.
