# S7A — authentic working 12-layer CALIBRI 43.26M → matched dense FP32 → same-shape reconversion

## Motivation
The separate 135M experiment S2–S6 demonstrated an extreme structural/functional mismatch between independently pretrained 30×576 SmolLM2 and naive uniform 5-bit/8weight CB59. **Before claiming a universal FP32→CALIBRI converter, validate a truly compatible 12×320 case against an authentic functioning CALIBRI source.** Freeze proven prior research weights and activate actual Green Tunnel, prompt route, copy and 12-layer generated adapter, NOT invented blank blocks.
