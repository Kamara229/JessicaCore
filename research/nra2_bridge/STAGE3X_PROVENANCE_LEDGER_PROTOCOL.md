Research-only design. This document is the S3V-LEDGER audit protocol, NOT the separate S3X reversal TRAIN128 preregistration in PR #639. **output gate=0**. No model inference, training, scoring, labels inspection, threshold changes or CI execution in this PR. This document is NOT evidence of successful validation.

## Problem
S3V `disagreement_or_error_cases` records only cases with incorrect soft/hard answers or answer disagreement. S3W fills omitted cases with correct source and target roles. That inference is unsound: both answers may be correct despite incorrect role predictions. S3W role/order slices are **not independently verified**. The original S3V producer does compute `correct_source` and `correct_destination` aggregates for `all`, each family and yes/no slices in `slice_metrics`; these are recorded producer-side aggregate measurements, **not** reconstructed individual role predictions or independently reproduced scores. The missing-row limitation applies to post-hoc order/wording slices and per-example attribution, not to the existence of those original aggregate counts.

## Exact producer predicate and safe partial bounds (static code audit)
S3V PR #637 emits a row iff `not soft_right[i] or not hard_right[i] or (soft_prediction[i] != hard_prediction[i])`. Therefore an omitted row establishes only that **both answer predictions were correct and agree**; it establishes nothing about either role. Before computing slices, validate recorded IDs are unique and in [0,63], each recorded row satisfies this predicate, and `omitted = 64 - recorded_unique`. Never infer role correctness for omitted rows.

For any predefined slice with `n` committed IDs, `k` observed rows and `u=n-k` omitted rows, let `s` be the number of observed rows with correct source and `t` the number with correct target. Report source accuracy **bounds** `[s/n, (s+u)/n]` and target accuracy bounds `[t/n, (t+u)/n]` (when `n>0`), plus `u`. For joint source-and-target correctness, use observed joint count `j` and bounds `[j/n,(j+u)/n]`. Do not report exact *post-hoc order/wording* role accuracy unless `u=0` or a separately validated full 64-row ledger exists. Original producer-side `correct_source`/`correct_destination` aggregate counts can be quoted as historical S3V metrics with their provenance clearly labeled, but they cannot establish order-specific role accuracy or independent reproduction. These bounds do not require guessing missing predictions and are not new candidate scores.
