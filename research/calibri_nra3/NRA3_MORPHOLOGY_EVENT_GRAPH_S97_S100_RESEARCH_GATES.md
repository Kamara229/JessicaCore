# Calibri 2.0 — NRA3 S97–S100: morphology before event graph

## Binding neural architecture constraint

NRA3 is part of the **same Calibri neural checkpoint**, not a separate external grammar engine. In all stages the child appears under `model.model.nra3_neural_weights.` in the original Calibri `state_dict`. The original compressed CB59/Green Tunnel neural core and its 12 transformer layers stay frozen in research. There are **eight distinct NRA2 neural mini-area heads** (including passive and negation), multi-layer routing, learned event→argument arcs and trainable in-weight event memory prototypes. No runtime UD parser is ever required.

## Why change the learning order?

On real original12-layer Calibri+GT hidden states, NRA3 S96 (#864) trained 22,311 new parameters on licensed UD-Taiga TRAIN-a: teacher-anchored event→argument top1 **238/317 = 75.08% TRAIN** but **17/110 = 15.45% same-corpus development**, versus random **2/110 = 1.82%**. **This is poor generalization and not production acceptance.** The S96 internal diagnostic is now spent; do not optimize against it.

The hypothesis is that inducing predicate-argument relationships without first learning robust Russian morphological properties makes structural transfer difficult. S97 adds explicit, *neural trainable* UPOS (17 classes), Case (9), Number (3), Voice (3), each from trainable multi-layer Calibri hidden features, plus trainable morphology-conditioned role-arc correction and relative original native-BPE distance bias. Original NRA3 S91 heads and all eight mini-areas are unchanged at initial zero correction. **Actual quality benefit is unproven until a new prospective test.**

## Mandatory acceptance gates

Research checkpoints are always `output_gate=0`, `candidate_accepted=false`, `activation_allowed=false`. Even if new UPOS and Case accuracy increases, this **does not imply** naturalistic event/factuality correctness, independent Russian syntax transfer or retained skills. Original historical 616 J7 are a baseline only; a newly integrated candidate must separately pass 616 J7 retention, online causal generation and full-stack bpp≤0.7 / CPU≥90% CB59 before *any* integration decision.
