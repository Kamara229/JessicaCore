On heldout realistic supported SciQ 24 rows (original FP32 teacher **20/24**), CB59 gave **6/24** and CB76 gave only **7/24**; on truncated BoolQ 28 rows, CB76 **10/28** vs original CB59 **16/28**. CB76 mean generated distinct token ratio only 9.7%, with a massive modal-token repetition share. **KL improvement has NOT restored useful semantic ability or generation**.

**Question v79:** can a true answer-conditioned functional loss applied directly to the original *compressed model* internal final hidden trajectories adjust exactly the **same physically paid rank4 tensor factors** and recover answer correctness on **independent** new answer questions without destroying WikiText/AGNews full-vocab KL or breaking CPU speed? A positive result would still not prove usable chat or generalization.

## Scientific decision
This is **actual supervised answer accuracy optimization of a paid 4D neural functional correction**, NOT full internal 135M dimensional weight training, and its new correction does not yet address early transformer-layer error propagation. Results may reveal overfitting on short SciQ/BoolQ TRAIN labels, poorer answer accuracy on new TEST, unchanged extreme repetition, or no candidate passing TRAIN VALID gates. ALL are valid negative observations. A true success requires independent answer QA better than v76 and original CB59 with appropriately scoped bootstrap/McNemar uncertainty, heldout full-model KL/NLL not catastrophically regressed, and no severe output repetition. Small tests are insufficient for claims of practical assistant competency.

No source weights from this project are to be deployed to production Jessica. The final report must distinguish actual native C 5bit base + Torch paid correction, report real whole archive bytes, and state exact confidence limits.
